We are happy to announce that **“Convergence of Local SGD under Heterogeneous Data”**, by Pham Quoc Anh, Tran Minh Khoa and Elena Rossi, has been accepted at **AAAI 2026**.

Main contribution: a new convergence bound for Local SGD that improves the term depending on data heterogeneity across clients. Code and the preprint are linked from the paper page.
