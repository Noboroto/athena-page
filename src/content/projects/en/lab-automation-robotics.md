## Goals

Reduce the number of real-world interactions needed to teach a robot arm pick-and-place operations on lab samples.

## Approach

Model-based reinforcement learning that reuses past trajectories through importance weighting.

## Results

The journal paper [Sample-Efficient Reinforcement Learning for Robot Manipulation](/en/papers/sample-efficient-rl-robot-manipulation/) and one PhD thesis defended in 2026.
