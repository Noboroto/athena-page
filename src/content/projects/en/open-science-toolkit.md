## Goals

Provide tooling that helps research groups publish code, data and experiment results in a reproducible way.

## Approach

Combine small locally-run language models with automation that records experiment configuration and environment.

## Results

The preprint [Lightweight Language Models for Open Scientific Workflows](/en/papers/lightweight-language-models-open-science/) and a public code release on GitHub.
