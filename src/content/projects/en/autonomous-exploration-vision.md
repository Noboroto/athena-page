## Goals

Build a perception stack that lets autonomous robots estimate depth, segment terrain and follow natural-language instructions with little labelled data.

## Approach

Self-supervised learning from image and LiDAR sequences, combined with a multimodal model that grounds instructions in observations.

## Results

Two published papers: [Self-Supervised Depth Estimation for Planetary Terrain](/en/papers/self-supervised-depth-planetary-terrain/) and [Multimodal Grounding of Natural-Language Instructions for Mobile Robots](/en/papers/multimodal-grounding-robot-instructions/).
